   account. Passing tests does not cover it. Needs a regression that fails on this baseline first
   (`ADAPTATION.md` section 3).
2. Networking, DTOs and auth are still BookOrbit-shaped and talk to no Litara server. They are temporary and
   pending replacement by the T003 adapter; do not extend them.
3. Notices: fflate and zip.js versions and the exact foliate-js revisions are unidentified; authorship of
   the BookOrbit-modified Foliate files and the React Native origin of `bridge.js` are unresolved. These are
   not relabelled MIT. No transitive license report exists.
4. Not visually checked: notification icon, themed monochrome icon, dark mode, login-screen logo.
5. The CI workflow is untested on GitHub; it needs the root `.github` to be merged first.
6. Media3 stays pinned at 1.5.1 with Cast workarounds; no dependency was upgraded.
