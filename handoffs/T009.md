## Milestone 15: API compatibility policy and enforcement (ADR-0082)
- ADR-0082 (Accepted on the user's "go with your defaults"): additive-only within v1, development until the first release with explicit waivers, immutable released snapshots, enforcement through `go test`. The support window after a v2 is 12 months (ADR-0082).
- `apps/server/internal/apicompat`: a direction-aware OpenAPI differ (requests may only accept more, responses may only promise more), a state/ledger/waiver record built into the binary, and `ctl api-baseline check|update|release`.
