## BLOCKER (needs a user decision; ADR edits are outside T005 scope)

Pristine Foliate loads every section into a child iframe (`sandbox="allow-same-origin allow-scripts"`, blob: src) and reads `contentDocument`. Inside the ADR-0067 opaque-origin sandbox `contentDocument` is null for such children in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (nested sandboxes inherit the missing allow-same-origin; srcdoc rerouting also fails). Foliate then throws (`reading 'documentElement'`/`'head'`) and never loads. Consequently EPUB, CBZ, FB2 reflowable/comic reading through pristine Foliate is NOT working; the 9 Foliate tests are marked `test.fail` and demonstrate this. Bounded zip loading, hostile-script and external-image blocking were observed (CSP console errors) but cannot be claimed as passing end to end. I did not add allow-same-origin, a second origin/host or patch upstream. Options needing an ADR decision: (a) separate reader origin (ADR-0067's rejected alternative), (b) a Litara-owned renderer using Foliate's book parsers but not its iframe paginator (no longer "pristine renderer"), (c) another engine for reflowable content.

PDF note: an opaque-origin document cannot start module workers (blob or cross-origin), and the PDF.js worker uses `import.meta`, so PDF.js runs its main-thread fake worker inside the sandbox frame. Not vendored: cmaps/standard fonts/wasm.
