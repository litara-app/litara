Gilt folio · three style variations

Same layout in all three (the one you chose), with only the skin changed: colour of the ground, how the panels are drawn, what the labels and quotes are set in. Small shared refinements: dotted leaders in the details, tabs as a gold underline, icon rail under the cover. Hover a cover to see the three-dot button.

A · Midnight gilt

Today's look, polished: black-brown leather, translucent panels with a double hairline gold frame, Fraunces labels. The most understated.

B · Oxblood and vellum

Deep red leather ground with the information on vellum pages, set in ink with oxblood labels. Easiest to read in long sessions; the paper panels make the data the brightest thing on screen.

C · Emerald and brass

Library-lamp green with brass. Panels have no box, just two brass corner brackets; labels and synopsis use the old-book typeface (IM Fell). The most distinctive and the most "antique".

Pick one (or mix: for instance B's vellum panels with C's brackets).
