B. Title page: four layouts

Same content in all four: cover, title, author, series, Read / Listen / Download / Add to shelf, then Details, Files, Highlights, Reading log and Edit metadata. Only the arrangement changes.

1 · Frontispiece

Cover left, title block right in the style of a title page, tabs across the full width below. The simplest and the most like today's page.

2 · Open book

A two-page spread. The left page is the cover and facts; the right page holds the tabs. Strongest book metaphor; needs width, so on a phone the pages stack.

3 · Catalog drawer

A library catalog card holds the cover and facts in typewriter-style ruled lines; the sections are index tabs on the right edge of a big sheet.

4 · Leather banner

A dark leather banner with the title in gold foil, the cover hanging over its lower edge, then content in two columns: tabs in the middle, a facts column on the right. Best for dense pages.
