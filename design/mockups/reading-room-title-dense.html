Reading Room · dense title page

Everything on one screen: rating, status, tags, synopsis, review, reading sessions, the full details list (publisher to dates finished), editions and audiobook, files, and a rail of more books by the author. Option 1 is the leather banner, kept on paper. Options 2–4 are new and lean into gilt (dark leather, gold hairlines, double-ruled panels). Each is drawn 1400px wide and shrunk to fit; hover a cover to see the three-dot menu appear.

Icon-only buttons (with tooltip and accessible name): mark read, download, add to shelf, send to device, more.Labelled buttons: Read, Listen, Write review.

1 · Leather banner, dense

The banner holds identity and actions: title in gold foil, rating, status, tags and the main buttons, with the cover hanging over its edge and a quick stats block on the right. Content below sits on paper in three columns: cover column with the facts, tabs and the prose in the middle, and the details and editions plates on the right.

2 · Gilt folio

A dark page of gilt-ruled plates. Left: the cover with the action rail beneath it as icon buttons. Middle: title, rating and status, then the synopsis, review and reading plates. Right: the details as a ledger with dotted leaders, then editions. Tabs run along the top bar like the reference.

3 · Gilt dossier

A horizontal data strip right under the title holds all ten facts at once (publisher, published, language, pages, ISBN, size, library, added, started, finished), so nothing needs a side column. Below it: synopsis and review, then reading sessions and files, then editions with the audiobook.

4 · Gilt marginalia

A paper page set into the dark leather, ruled in gold: cover and facts on the left leaf, the text in the middle, and the details as handwritten-style margin notes on the right. Actions live in a narrow gilt-edged gutter as icon buttons. The most bookish and the quietest to read, with the same information.
