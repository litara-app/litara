# Litara brand mark

## Selected design

On November 12, 2026, the repository owner selected **04 Bookmark** from the
initial logo exploration: a blue bookmark ribbon with an open-book cutout.
The owner explored the Spine L and bookshelf L alternatives, then explicitly
returned to the bookmark concept and requested that it be recorded in this repo.

[logo.svg](logo.svg) is the editable vector master of that selected symbol.
It recreates the chosen concept as simple vector geometry, rather than embedding
or automatically tracing the generated concept image. The book is a transparent
cutout, so the background shows through. The split between the pages remains blue.

## Appearance and use

- Primary color: **#228be6**, retaining continuity with Litara's original blue.
- Rounded top corners, a centered ribbon notch, and a symmetric open book.
- Transparent background; square 256 by 256 view box with built-in clear space.
- No fonts, external resources, raster images, gradients, or scripts.
- Preserve proportions and cutouts. A single-color rendition can change the path
  fill to suit its background while retaining sufficient contrast.
- The asset contains only the symbol. The concept board's wordmark typography
  is not a separately selected or licensed font specification.

This records a visual design choice, not an application architectural decision.
Adding the master here does not wire it into future web, Android, documentation,
favicon, or launcher assets. Platform-specific exports and launch-screen treatment
remain follow-up integration work.

## Provenance

The concept originated in the AI-generated Litara logo exploration in this chat;
the SVG was redrawn for this repository. No third-party logo or font assets were
imported. It is first-party artwork under the repository's MIT licensing direction.
